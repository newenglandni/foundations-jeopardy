Quick start

How to Play

  1. Choose a level. Level 1 covers Part 1, Level 2 covers Part 2, and Level 3 covers Part 3.
  2. Select a category and point value to open a clue.
  3. Listen to the narrated question, or use Read Question Aloud if you need to hear it again.
  4. When the answer appears, mark Correct to add the clue value or Incorrect to subtract it. Scores cannot go below zero.
  5. Close the clue after scoring. Used clues are marked with a check and cannot be played again.
  6. You have 5 seconds to answer. The game announces time’s up and reveals the answer automatically when the timer reaches zero.
  7. Incorrect and timed-out questions are saved for a study review that appears after all clues have been played.
  8. After the answer is read aloud, it remains on screen for five seconds before the next clue is ready.

Audio: Narration starts when a clue opens. Music and sound effects may be muted with the Music button. Use Reset Board to start over.
